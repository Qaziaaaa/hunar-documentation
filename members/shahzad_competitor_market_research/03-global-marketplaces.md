# 03 — Global Marketplace Research

## 1. Taskrabbit

Taskrabbit is useful for understanding a structured marketplace with provider profiles, rates, identity checks, support and cancellation rules.

### Customer / pricing model

Customers can see Tasker hourly rates, and Taskers set their own rates. Taskrabbit also applies service and Trust & Support fees. [Taskrabbit](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/46260499891355-How-Much-Does-it-Cost-to-Hire-a-Tasker) [Fees](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/46260411872155-What-s-the-Taskrabbit-Service-Fee)

### Trust model

Taskrabbit requires identity verification for Taskers and, in the US, its trust process includes identity and criminal background checks. [Identity verification](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/46260392641179-Identity-Verification-Process) [Trust & Safety](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/46260491906203-Overview-of-Trust-and-Safety)

### Cancellation

Taskrabbit has explicit cancellation fees in defined circumstances, including some late customer cancellations. The policy also accounts for cases where the Tasker does not show. [Cancellation policy](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/46260411471899-Cancellation-Policy)

### What to learn

- Provider-controlled pricing
- Strong verification
- Clear fee display
- Formal cancellation rules
- Customer support / protection

### What to improve for our context

Use a simpler local model. A home-repair marketplace in Pakistan should avoid fee complexity during MVP.

## 2. Airtasker

Airtasker is a direct marketplace where customers post tasks and Taskers make offers. Airtasker says the platform supports secure payments, insurance, dispute resolution and support. [How it works](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360021876472-How-to-get-started-on-Airtasker)

### Offers and matching

Customers can review offers and consider profile, reviews, completion rate and verification badges before selecting a Tasker. [Safety tips](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-us/articles/202402924-What-are-some-safety-tips-I-should-follow-after-posting-a-task)

### Payments

After assignment, payment can be held through Airtasker Pay, and customers can increase the task price if more payment is mutually agreed. [Safety tips](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-us/articles/202402924-What-are-some-safety-tips-I-should-follow-after-posting-a-task)

### Fees

Airtasker documents a service-fee model for Taskers; one published support page states a range of 12.5%–20% depending on tier. Fees are shown to the Tasker when making an offer. [Service fee](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-us/articles/200294499-What-is-the-service-fee)

### Dispute handling

Airtasker has a three-stage dispute process: attempt resolution, platform review of evidence, then a platform recommendation/decision. [Dispute process](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001291168-What-is-Airtasker-s-dispute-process)

### What to learn

- Customer posts a real-world task
- Multiple providers can make offers
- Choice is not based on price alone
- Payment can be protected
- Price can be changed with explicit agreement
- Disputes rely on evidence

This is one of the closest conceptual references for our marketplace.

## 3. Thumbtack

Thumbtack presents a simpler consumer experience:

`Search → Review profiles → Chat about price/project → Hire`

Customers can browse professionals, read verified reviews, compare pricing information and communicate before hiring. [Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/how-it-works)

### What to learn

- Strong profile comparison
- Reviews as a selection tool
- Price discussion before hiring
- Simple customer journey

### Limitation for our model

Thumbtack's search-and-hire pattern is less directly focused on the immediate “post a repair problem and wait for nearby offers” mechanism.

---

## Global conclusion

The strongest reusable concepts are:

1. **Airtasker:** job posting + provider offers + protected payment + dispute process.
2. **Taskrabbit:** provider profiles + verification + structured pricing + cancellation rules.
3. **Thumbtack:** profile/review comparison + simple customer decision-making.
4. **inDrive:** negotiated price + customer/provider choice.
