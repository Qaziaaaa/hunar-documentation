# 06 — Shahzad's Recommendations

## COPY

- Provider choice
- Negotiated offers
- Verified profiles
- Ratings/reviews
- Job posting with images/details
- Price comparison
- Clear cancellation rules
- Structured dispute process
- Transaction history

## IMPROVE

- Separate visit fee from repair estimate
- Require explicit approval for price changes
- Show relevant provider signals beside every offer
- Keep agreement evidence inside the platform
- Make complaints traceable to the exact booking
- Rank nearby workers by a balanced set of signals

## UNIQUE OPPORTUNITY

### Two-stage negotiation

**Before visit:** customer and worker agree on visit/inspection charge.

**After inspection:** worker submits repair estimate; customer approves, rejects or negotiates.

This is the central product-gap hypothesis coming from the competitive research.

## DO NOT BUILD

- Full super-app
- Unrelated services
- Pure directory
- Cheapest-price-only ranking
- Uncontrolled/off-platform price changes
- Complex features that do not improve the core home-service transaction

## MVP competitive priority

The first product should prove one thing:

> Can a customer post a real home-service problem and reliably select a suitable nearby worker through transparent offers and a clearly controlled price/repair process?

Everything else should be secondary until that is validated.
