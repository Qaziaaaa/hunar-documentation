Frequently Asked Questions

How does HUNAR protect my escrow money? expand_more

Your payment is held in a protected bank escrow vault. Funds are only transferred to the service professional after you inspect the completed job and approve release via SMS verification code. Filing a dispute instantly freezes payout.

What is the 5-day craftsmanship guarantee? expand_more

If the resolved work fails, leaks, or malfunctions within 5 calendar days of job sign-off, HUNAR mandates the original technician to return free of charge, or dispatches a Senior Pro from our Peshawar network with no additional fee.

How long does the investigation take? expand_more

Our Peshawar operations desk acknowledges every ticket within 2 hours. Technicians have 3 business hours to provide their defense or evidence. If unanswered or verified faulty, escrow funds are automatically credited to your wallet.

Can I request a cash refund instead of wallet credit? expand_more

Yes. Wallet refunds reflect instantly. If you paid via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or Raast/Debit card, you may request our desk to route funds back to your original source account within 24–48 banking hours.
