# 02 — Pakistan & Local Market Research

## 1. Mahir Company

Mahir Company describes itself as an online marketplace connecting service providers and seekers. It offers home maintenance, cleaning and personal care, and says providers go through verification/training before joining its partner ecosystem. [Mahir Company](https://mahircompany.com/about-us)

Its current public materials advertise 200+ services and categories including plumbing, electrician, AC technician, carpenter, painter, geyser, handyman and appliance services. The app listing also states that professionals are screened/vetted and that the company targets fast arrival for bookings. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mrmahir.customer)

### Business model pattern

- Service catalog
- Customer booking
- Provider assignment
- Company-managed service experience
- Verified-provider positioning
- Customer support
- Multiple home/personal-care categories

### Strengths to learn from

- Trust/verification is visible.
- Large service catalog.
- Managed booking is easier for customers than finding a worker manually.
- The model is already localized to Pakistan.

### Important customer pain signals

Public app reviews include complaints about pricing changing after inspection, weak service resolution, slow support, and service quality. These are individual reviews and should not be treated as statistically representative of all bookings. [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/mahir-company-home-beauty/id1576178647?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews) [Google Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mrmahir.customer)

### Product-gap insight

A home-services platform should make the **price transition from “visit” to “repair” extremely explicit**.

Example:

`Visit fee → Inspection → Written estimate → Customer approval → Repair → Completion`

That can directly address the type of pricing confusion visible in public reviews.

## 2. Services Master

Services Master advertises home services including plumbing, electrical, AC repair and carpentry. It also advertises real-time tracking and multiple payment options including wallet, card and cash on delivery. [Services Master](https://servicesmaster.com.pk/)

### Strengths to learn

- Broad service categories
- Provider tracking
- Multiple payment methods
- Local Pakistani positioning

### Product-gap insight

Tracking and payment convenience are useful, but the proposed product should differentiate primarily through **better job negotiation, clearer repair estimates and marketplace choice**, not by trying to win only on number of categories.

---

## 3. KAAM

KAAM presents itself as a Pakistani verified-services marketplace. Its local-services section includes electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, cleaners and other providers. Its described workflow is:

`Tell us the job → Compare providers → Book/message → Complete and review`

It also asks providers to submit profiles for verification. [KAAM](https://kaaam.pk/)

### Strengths

- Job + area + budget + urgency
- Provider comparison
- Verification
- Multiple marketplace categories

### Product-gap insight

This model validates that **posting a job + comparing providers** is a natural marketplace pattern in Pakistan.

The proposed product can go one step further by making the customer request richer: description + images + voice + location + preferred time + visit-charge offer/negotiation.

---

## 4. Kaam Waala

Kaam Waala is a Pakistan App Store listing centered on handyman requests. The customer can post a service request, upload details/images, set a bid, and receive responses through the app. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/kaam-waala/id6756620386)

### Why this competitor is especially relevant

Its workflow is conceptually close to the proposed platform:

`Post request → Add details/images → Set bid → Receive response`

This confirms that bidding is not merely a ride-hailing idea; it is already being used as a home/handyman request pattern.

### Product-gap insight

The platform should distinguish itself beyond “post a bid” by adding:

- Provider comparison signals
- Verification level
- Estimated arrival
- Visit charge vs repair estimate
- Negotiation history
- Customer approval checkpoints
- Better dispute evidence

---

## 5. KaamKrew

KaamKrew describes a home-maintenance and beauty service app with services such as home cleaning, AC repair and electrical work, connecting customers with qualified/skilled workers. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/kaamkrew/id6477297803)

### Lesson

The Pakistani market already contains specialist and broad-service booking products. A new product needs a distinctive transaction model, not just another directory of service providers.

---

## Local market conclusion

The Pakistan market shows three recurring approaches:

| Model | Example | Main value |
|---|---|---|
| Managed booking | Mahir Company | Convenience + verified providers |
| Compare/book marketplace | KAAM | Provider choice |
| Bid/request marketplace | Kaam Waala | Customer-defined job + bid |

### Opportunity for our product

Combine the strongest parts without becoming unnecessarily complex:

`Detailed job request + nearby qualified providers + comparable offers + negotiated visit fee + post-inspection repair estimate + approval + protected completion/review`
